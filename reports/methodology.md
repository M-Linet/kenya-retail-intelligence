# Methodology

## 1. Data acquisition

### Public datasets reviewed
| Dataset | What it offers | Why it was not used directly |
|---|---|---|
| UCI **Online Retail II** (id 502, CC BY 4.0) | 1,067,371 real invoice lines, Dec 2009 – Dec 2011, from a UK online gift-ware retailer (many customers are wholesalers) | No branches, product cost, inventory, promotions or categories; priced in GBP; non-store e-commerce. Gross margin and inventory questions cannot be answered from it. |
| Kaggle supermarket-sales style datasets | Small samples with branch and product-line fields | Usually about 1,000 rows over 3 months with no cost or inventory history and unclear provenance. Too thin for turnover or year-over-year analysis. |
| KNBS / CBK public statistics | Real Kenyan macro and retail context | Aggregates only; no transactions. |

### Decision
A **reproducible synthetic dataset** for a **fictional** chain (*Savanna Fresh Supermarkets*), generated by `src/generate_data.py` (seed 2027). Nothing in it represents a real retailer, brand or customer. All brand names are invented.

### What the generator encodes, and what it does not
| Encoded (an assumption I chose) | Left to emerge (measured, not set) |
|---|---|
| Category margin ranges, brand price tiers | Exact margins by product, branch and month |
| Weekday, payday, December and Easter seasonality; 8%/year trend | Size of each effect in the realised data |
| One branch with discretionary discounting; two with 2-week supplier lead time; one with falling footfall; one opened mid-period | Which KPIs reveal them, by how much, and what they cost |
| A reorder-point policy with some stale reorder levels and over-ordered SKUs | Stockout rates, lost sales, slow-moving value |
| Category-specific promotion response | Whether each promotion pays back |
| Data-quality defects (logged in `injection_manifest.json`) | Whether the cleaning rules catch them without false positives |

**Why this matters in an interview:** because I designed the business mechanisms, finding them is not a discovery about the world. What the project does demonstrate is that the pipeline, the metric definitions and the diagnostic method recover them correctly and size them, including effects I did not set directly, such as the Household & Cleaning decline, which came from the demand collapse interacting with replenishment.

### Realism choices
- **Inventory is consistent with sales.** Weekly stock is simulated from demand: when stock runs out, the excess demand is removed from the sales lines. About 4.9% of demanded units were lost this way. Lost demand is invisible in the sales data, as in real POS systems.
- **Anonymous baskets** (38%) have no customer ID, as in a real loyalty programme.
- **Small Business customers** buy staples in bulk (up to about 50 units), which creates genuine outliers that cleaning must not delete.

## 3. Cleaning rules (applied in `src/data_cleaning.py`, every action logged)
| Problem | Rule | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Exact duplicate rows (items, receipts, customers, stock) | drop the copy; stop the run if the same key has different content | 3,604 / 1,159 / 90 / 498 |
| Non-standard category, segment and payment labels | map to a canonical list (case, whitespace and '&'/'and' insensitive); stop on unmapped labels | 18 / 150 / 13,819 |
| Missing payment method | `Unknown` | 5,797 |
| Timestamps in 2052/2062 | quarantine with their lines (the correct digit would be a guess) | 21 receipts |
| Receipts for an unknown branch (BR99) | quarantine with their lines | 40 receipts |
| Quantity ≥ 100 on a non-business basket | quarantine (×100 keying pattern; genuine bulk buying peaks at about 50) | 60 lines |
| Negative quantity | move to `retail.returns` | 2,996 lines |
| Zero unit price | impute the product's modal shelf price and flag `price_imputed` | 35 lines |
| Missing unit cost | category median cost ratio × modal price; flag `unit_cost_imputed` | 3 products |
| Negative closing stock | set to 0 | 50 snapshots |
| Promotion end date before start date | swap the dates | 2 promotions |
| Placeholder registration date 1900-01-01 | NULL | 7 customers |

Net effect: raw gross line value of KES 496.7M fell to KES 494.3M after cleaning. Without cleaning, sales would have been overstated by 0.5%, mostly by 60 typo lines and the duplicates.

## 4. Metric definitions
| Metric | Definition | Pitfall avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | Σ qty × price − discount | Shelf prices are treated as VAT-exclusive (an assumption). Returns are excluded and reported separately. |
| Gross margin % | Σ GP ÷ Σ revenue | Never an average of line or branch margins |
| ATV | revenue ÷ distinct receipts (from `v_baskets`) | Counting lines instead of receipts |
| Like-for-like growth | Year 2 vs Year 1, branches open for both full years | Counting the new Ruiru branch as growth |
| Day-of-week averages | revenue ÷ number of that weekday in the calendar | Saturdays occurring 105 times vs Mondays 104 times |
| Stockout rate | share of branch-product-weeks with closing stock 0 | Snapshots capture end-of-week only, so mid-week stockouts that recovered are missed |
| Turnover | COGS ÷ average weekly stock at cost × 52 ÷ weeks | Valuing stock at retail price; summing balances across weeks |
| Promotion incremental GP | promo GP − (28-day full-price baseline GP/day × days) | Cannibalisation, pantry loading and footfall are not measured, so this is a screen, not a causal estimate |
| Lost GP from stockouts | stockout weeks × the pair's average in-stock weekly GP | Approximate; true demand during a stockout is unknown |

## 5. Analytical techniques
SQL: joins, CTEs, subqueries, `CASE WHEN`, conditional aggregation (`FILTER`), window functions (`LAG`, `RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER`, `NTILE`, running and moving `SUM`/`AVG`), date arithmetic and views.
Python: distribution analysis (log scales, quantiles), Spearman correlation, Welch's t-test (Nyali's monthly margin against the other branches), outlier investigation by segment, and two forecasting baselines (seasonal naive and moving average, evaluated by holdout MAPE) as an optional extension.

## 6. Validation
- 11 integrity checks on the clean layer, all returning 0 (`dq_b01`); no join fan-out (`dq_b02`).
- Detection counts match the generator's injection manifest exactly (notebook 02).
- `pytest`: 39 tests covering cleaning rules, metric definitions, PK/FK integrity, and SQL-vs-pandas revenue and gross profit reconciliation to 1e-9.
- The whole project was rebuilt from an empty database with `scripts/run_all.py` and produced identical figures.
